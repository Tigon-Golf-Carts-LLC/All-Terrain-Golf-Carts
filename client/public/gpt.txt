## INDUSTRY TERMINOLOGY

- LSV: Low Speed Vehicle - street-legal vehicle limited to 25 MPH
- 4X4: Four-wheel drive with power to all wheels
- Golf Cart: Small vehicle originally designed for golf courses
- NEV: Neighborhood Electric Vehicle (similar to LSV)
- Range: Distance traveled on single battery charge
- Regenerative Braking: System that recharges battery while braking
- Lithium Battery: Advanced battery technology with longer life
